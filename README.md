# Adithya Krishnan

I'm a first-year MBBS student at Barts and The London, interested in cardiology and cardiac data science.

I study what happens when a medical AI model meets different patients. Good ranking does not necessarily mean reliable predictions.

## Research projects

- **calibration-shift**: a Python package for checking calibration alongside discrimination and applying a prevalence-shift correction. The correction has limits; it does not repair every kind of dataset shift. Code: [link].
- **fundus-cimt-calibration-audit**: a reproducible audit of the public China-Fundus-CIMT benchmark. An age-and-sex model predicting thickened neck-artery walls retained an AUC of 0.8947 but predicted about 39% more cases than observed in a group where the condition was less common. A one-number correction repaired most, but not all, of the gap. Code: [link].
- **ecg-age-gap-bias**: a replication of age-gap bias correction on 233,647 patients from CODE-15%. Correcting for chronological age reversed the crude mortality comparison. This is a replication, not a new biomarker discovery or evidence of a causal effect. Code: [link].

These projects are for research and evaluation. They are not medical devices and are not for clinical use.

I'm interested in working with researchers on clinical prediction, calibration, ECG AI and cardiac modelling.
